# Voice Capture research

Research snapshot on 2026-11-24. Commercial product findings describe documented behavior. Global activation time, first audio, stop latency, full keyboard access and VoiceOver were either undocumented or outside the hands-on comparison for those products. Vendor claims and public reports are identified below. Rasc timings come from the separate local experiment.

Across these tools, motor effort, privacy and recoverability matter more than the number of shortcut modes. Global paste changes permissions and introduces target-app races. Rasc owns the write destination. Vendor demos and changelogs can establish interaction patterns and known failure classes. They cannot establish comparative latency on the same Mac. None of the documented confidence or alternate-word affordances establish preservation of intent.

| **SpeechAnalyzer + SpeechTranscriber** | Swift concurrency API introduced macOS 26. Check `isAvailable`, `supportedLocale(equivalentTo:)` and asset status at runtime. `en_US` and `pt_BR` observed on M3 Pro. Explicit one-locale module. ## System contracts to use

- Microphone access needs a contextual `AVCaptureDevice.requestAccess(for: .audio)`, a bundle `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription`, and the tested sandbox/hardened runtime `com.apple.security.device.audio-input`. Check current Xcode-generated entitlements and exact shipping signing configuration. System audio capture has a different usage key and sits outside this feature. [Apple media authorization](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/requesting-authorization-for-media-capture-on-macos) · [App Sandbox entitlement reference](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/Miscellaneous/Reference/EntitlementKeyReference/Chapters/EnablingAppSandbox.html)
- Apple documents SpeechAnalyzer as on-device with separate managed asset downloads. The signed probe used both transcribers while legacy speech permission was undetermined and the speech usage key was absent. The old authorization article explicitly covers an API that may use servers. Verify the candidate across clean builds and releases before wording a promise about OS services. [SpeechAnalyzer session](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/277/) · [Legacy authorization](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/speech/asking-permission-to-use-speech-recognition)
- Ad-hoc signing can produce a designated requirement tied to a CDHash. A matching bundle ID alone is insufficient evidence for TCC continuity. Sparkle's Ed25519 update signature and library loader constraint govern different trust decisions. [Apple TN3127](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/technotes/tn3127-inside-code-signing-requirements) · [Sparkle signing guide](https://sparkle-project.org/documentation/)
