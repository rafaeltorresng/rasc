# Community conduct

Rasc welcomes constructive participation from people of all backgrounds.

- Discuss the work with respect; critique decisions and code, not people.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct and personal attacks.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish someone else's personal information or thoughts.
- Give actionable feedback and allow room for disagreement and correction.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests and other project spaces.
The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation.
Report concerns privately to **rafaeltorresng@gmail.com**; include only the details
needed to explain the incident. Reports are handled discreetly, without a promise
of absolute confidentiality or a fixed response timeline.
